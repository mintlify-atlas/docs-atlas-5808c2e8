### Repayment Processing

The repayment engine supports flexible payment allocation strategies.

**Location:** `server/opencbs-loans/src/main/java/com/opencbs/loans/services/repayment/`

<Accordion title="Repayment Split Algorithm">
  The repayment split algorithm determines how payments are allocated:
  
  1. **Calculate Outstanding** - Determine all amounts due
  2. **Apply Payment Order** - Allocate by priority:
     - Penalties
     - Late fees
     - Interest
     - Principal
  3. **Distribute Across Installments** - Allocate to oldest first
  4. **Handle Overpayment** - Store as advance or unallocated
  5. **Generate Accounting Entries** - Create double-entry records
</Accordion>
