The loan schedule is a detailed breakdown of all expected repayment installments, showing principal, interest, fees, and payment dates for the life of the loan.

## Viewing Loan Schedules

Loan schedules can be accessed for both loan applications and disbursed loans.

### For Loan Applications

View the projected schedule before disbursement:
```
GET /loan-applications/{id}/schedule
```

Access via:
- Open the loan application
- Navigate to the **Schedule** tab
- Review projected installments

### For Disbursed Loans

View the active loan schedule:
```
GET /loans/{id}/schedule
```

Access via:
- Open the loan details page
- Click on the **Schedule** tab
- View all installments and payment status

## Schedule Components

Each installment in the schedule includes:

| Component | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Number** | Installment sequence number |
| **Due Date** | Date payment is due |
| **Principal** | Principal amount due |
| **Interest** | Interest amount due |
| **Fees** | Any applicable fees |
| **Total** | Total payment due |
| **Outstanding Balance** | Remaining principal after payment |
| **Paid** | Whether installment has been paid |
| **Paid Date** | Actual payment date |
| **Paid Principal** | Amount of principal paid |
| **Paid Interest** | Amount of interest paid |

<Note>
  The schedule automatically calculates all amounts based on the loan product configuration, interest rate, term, and disbursement date.
</Note>

## Schedule Calculation Methods

OpenCBS Cloud supports multiple schedule calculation methods:

### Equal Installments (Annuity)
- Fixed payment amount each period
- Principal increases, interest decreases over time
- Most common for consumer loans

### Equal Principal
- Fixed principal amount each period
- Interest calculated on declining balance
- Total payment decreases over time

### Flat Interest
- Interest calculated on original principal
- Same interest amount each period
- Common in microfinance

### Declining Balance
- Interest calculated on remaining balance
- More accurate interest calculation
- Industry standard method

<Tip>
  The calculation method is configured in the loan product. Different products can use different methods based on your institution's policies.
</Tip>

## Grace Periods

Loans may include grace periods:

### Principal Grace Period
- Only interest payments during grace period
- Principal payments begin after grace period ends
- Extends total loan term

### Interest Grace Period
- No interest accrues during grace period
- Reduces total interest paid
- Less common in practice

## Rescheduling Loans

Loans can be rescheduled to modify the payment schedule:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Validate Reschedule">
    Before rescheduling, validate the proposed changes:
    ```
    PUT /loans/{id}/reschedule/validate
    ```

    This endpoint checks:
    - New terms are within allowed parameters
    - No pending payments or violations
    - User has appropriate permissions
  </Step>

  <Step title="Apply Schedule Update">
    Update the loan schedule:
    ```
    PUT /loans/{id}/schedule-update
    ```

    Provide:
    - Modified installment details
    - Reason for rescheduling
    - Any applicable fees
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm Changes">
    Review the updated schedule:
    - Verify new installment amounts
    - Check updated due dates
    - Confirm outstanding balance calculations
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Rescheduling may require special permissions and approval workflows depending on your institution's policies.
</Note>

## Schedule Status Indicators

Installments are marked with different statuses:

- **Pending** - Future payment not yet due
- **Due** - Payment is due (within current period)
- **Overdue** - Payment past due date
- **Paid** - Payment received in full
- **Partial** - Partial payment received
- **Late** - Paid after due date

## Accrued Interest

View accrued interest between payment dates:

- Interest accrues daily based on outstanding principal
- Displayed on the loan detail page
- Updated in real-time
- Included in next payment amount

## Fees in the Schedule

The schedule may include various fees:

### Periodic Fees
- Charged on specific installments
- Can be monthly, quarterly, or one-time
- Examples: account maintenance, insurance

### Entry Fees
- One-time fees at disbursement
- May be capitalized into loan or paid separately
- Examples: origination fee, processing fee

### Penalty Fees
- Applied for late payments
- Calculated based on days overdue
- Added to scheduled amounts

## Prepayment Impact

When clients make early or extra payments:

### Prepayment Application
Payments can be applied to:
1. Fees and penalties first
2. Accrued interest
3. Principal (reduces balance)

### Schedule Adjustment Options
- **Reduce installment amount** - Keep same term, lower payments
- **Reduce term** - Keep same payment, finish earlier
- **One-time reduction** - Credit to next installment

<Tip>
  Prepayment policies are configured in the loan product settings. Some products may charge early repayment fees.
</Tip>

## Viewing Payment History

Compare scheduled vs. actual payments:

- View payment transaction history
- See when payments were received
- Identify partial or late payments
- Track payment method used

## Schedule in Different Currencies

For multi-currency institutions:

- Schedule displays in loan currency
- Exchange rates fixed at disbursement
- Payments accepted in loan currency
- Conversion rates apply if paid in different currency

## Holidays and Non-Working Days

Schedule respects your institution's holiday calendar:

- Holidays configured in system settings
- Due dates auto-adjust to next business day
- Or previous business day (based on configuration)
- Grace periods may account for holidays

Holiday configuration:
```
GET /holidays
```

## Actualization and Accruals

<Note>
  **Last Actualization Date** is important for accurate interest calculations. The system tracks when interest was last calculated and accrued.
</Note>

When processing repayments, the system checks:
- Last actualization date
- Days since last accrual
- Current accrued interest
- Warns if dates don't match

## Best Practices

<Tip>
  **Schedule Management Tips**
  - Review schedules carefully before disbursement
  - Verify first payment date is correctly set
  - Check that installment amounts match client expectations
  - Ensure holiday calendar is up to date
  - Document any reschedule reasons thoroughly
  - Communicate schedule changes to clients immediately
</Tip>

## Common Questions

**Why doesn't my schedule match the expected payment?**
- Check grace periods applied
- Verify interest calculation method
- Review any fees included
- Confirm disbursement date

**Can I change an installment date?**
- Use the reschedule function
- Requires appropriate permissions
- May trigger approval workflow

**How are partial payments reflected?**
- Applied to fees, interest, then principal
- Installment marked as partially paid
- Remaining balance shown
- May affect subsequent installments
